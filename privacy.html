Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Clipper ("the App") handles information. Clipper is a personal, locally-hosted application used to automatically clip and post gaming content to social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

Clipper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to third parties. The App operates entirely on your local machine. No user data is sent to any external server operated by the App developer.

2. Third-Party Services

Clipper integrates with the following third-party platforms at the user's request:

  • TikTok: Used to upload video clips on behalf of the user. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to any data shared with TikTok.
  • YouTube: Used to upload video clips on behalf of the user. Google's privacy policy applies to any data shared with YouTube.
  • Google Gemini API: Used to generate captions for video clips. Google's privacy policy applies.

3. Video Content

Video files processed by Clipper are stored locally on the user's device. The App only uploads video content to TikTok or YouTube when explicitly instructed to do so by the user.

4. API Tokens and Keys

API keys and access tokens are stored locally in a configuration file on the user's device. They are never transmitted to any server other than the respective platform APIs they are intended for.

5. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of any changes.

7. Contact

This is a personal-use application. For questions, contact the developer directly via GitHub.
